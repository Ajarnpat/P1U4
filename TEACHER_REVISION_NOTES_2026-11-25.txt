AP PHYSICS 1 UNIT 4 — TEACHER REVISION NOTES (25 NOV 2026)

Revised teaching sequence for the Lesson 4.3 free-response answers.

1. The Lesson 4.3 worked-solutions PDF has been rewritten for all four FRQs.
   Every subpart begins with a principle or defining equation before substitutions,
   numerical calculation and interpretation. Equations have proper PDF subscripts.
2. The original handwritten car-lorry worked example supplied by Pat was read
   and followed. In particular: the two vehicles experience equal and opposite
   112,500 N average forces, yet the lighter 1200 kg car has three times the
   acceleration magnitude of the 3600 kg lorry (93.75 vs 31.25 m/s^2).
   Assuming both drivers move with their vehicles during the collision and
   have approximately equal masses, the smaller-car driver experiences three
   times the average net decelerating force.
3. For the student question (FRQ 4e) in both the coach and the questions PDF,
   the old requirement about injury prediction was intentionally replaced by
   this intended AP idealised comparison. Real-world caveats appear only in an
   explicitly optional side note in the teacher worked answers.
4. The numerical values, earlier FRQs 1-3, MCQs and the PhET/Google Sheet
   investigations are otherwise unchanged. In FRQ 3 the solutions explicitly
   note that the given data do not uniquely determine final velocity vectors.
5. The Lesson 4.3 worked-solutions PDF is linked from the online learning coach.

For future AP FRQ model answers, use: Principle -> substitutions -> conclusion.
