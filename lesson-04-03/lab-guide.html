Explore 1D collisions — 10-minute challenge

Collect as many collision results as you can in 10 minutes. Investigate patterns using PhET, and share your results with the class. You may return early or continue beyond the timer.

Step 1 · Investigate

PhET Collision Lab · 1D

Select the 1D experiment. Vary elasticity, both masses and both signed initial velocities. Explore the available buttons and displays.

Masses: 1–5 kg
Velocities: −5 to +5 m/s

Step 2 · Record

Shared class Google Sheet

  1. Open the Data Entry tab. Do not look at the Totals tabs yet.
  2. Before each collision, enter elasticity, both masses and both initial velocities in an empty row.
  3. Run the collision in PhET; then enter both final velocities in the same row.
  4. Repeat to contribute more results. Discuss the totals and patterns with your class when invited.
