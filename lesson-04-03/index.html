After Investigation 1 · What did you find?

Write your own observations first. Reveal each answer separately when you have made an attempt.

OBSERVATION A

What did you notice about the total momentum of both carts before and after the collision, no matter what collision type?

The total momentum of the two-cart system was the same before and after the collision, whether the collision was elastic, partially inelastic or perfectly inelastic (provided the external horizontal impulse was negligible).
OBSERVATION B

What did you notice about the total kinetic energy before the collision compared with after?

In an elastic collision the total kinetic energy was unchanged. In an inelastic collision it decreased; some kinetic energy was transferred to internal energy. In this idealised collision model, total kinetic energy did not increase.
OBSERVATION C

What did you notice about the velocity of the centre of mass before compared with after the collision? Can you explain this?

The centre of mass had the same velocity before, during and after the collision. The forces between the carts were internal to the two-cart system, and the net external horizontal force was negligible; therefore the system's total momentum, and hence its centre-of-mass velocity, stayed constant. If initially at rest, its centre of mass remained stationary.
